POWER BLADE

Levels are procedurally generated, so the game never ends. Reach the door at the end of each level, but kill every enemy first. Beat the clock! Press T to restart a level. Every other enemy you defeat asks a surface area question (the shapes get harder as levels go up): answer it to continue, each wrong answer costs +3s. Enemies stay on their platforms, and falling in the pit sends you back to the start. Throw your blade and let it ricochet: every bounce adds damage.

Click throw. Click near an enemy to slash instead. The blade curves slightly toward enemies, slows down over distance and drops. Go pick it up, or recall it with E / right-click. Catch it in the air and your bounce combo is kept until you land.

WASD move   Space jump / double jump   Shift slide (ground) / dive (air)   R roll
Throwing recoils you: a little on the ground, a lot in the air. In the air, Shift while facing the blade = dash to it (3 in a row, then 2.5s cooldown). Roll as you land from a dive or any fall over 4.5 units to keep all your speed (miss it and you stop for 0.5s). No knife in hand = +30% speed.

Arrows also look if mouse lock is unavailable.
