How it works: spend gold to place figures on grass tiles. Figures level up from duplicate pulls (+20% damage each) and can be upgraded in battle three times. Every 10th wave brings a warlord. Try Endless to see how many waves you can survive. Coins you earn buy more packs.

Summon figures

Odds: Common 60%, Rare 28%, Epic 9%, Legendary 3%. A Legendary is guaranteed within 40 pulls. Duplicates raise a figure's level up to 5. Ten-packs cost 9 times one pack and guarantee a Rare or better. Each pack reveals a figure's biography: answer the question about it correctly to recruit them, or they get away!
