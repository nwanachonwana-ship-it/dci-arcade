Launch Lab

A DCI Middle School ACE Club

What We Do

Launch Lab is an after-school club where students learn prompt engineering and software design by building real educational games — not toy projects, actual tools other students use every day.

Every game in the DCI Arcade was designed, built, tested, and shipped by a Launch Lab student team, start to finish.

Our Mission

We believe the fastest way to get students excited about math, science, and every other subject is to let other students build the games that teach it.

All apps are built by students, for students.

Leadership

Launch Lab was founded and is led by Nwanacho Nwana, a high school math teacher who started the club to give students hands-on experience with AI-assisted software design — while building something the whole school could actually use and enjoy.

Our Flagship Product

The DCI Arcade is Launch Lab's flagship product: a growing collection of student-built games, each with its own leaderboard and live Arcade Points standings.
